Privacy Policy for IdeaSwipe

Last updated: April 19, 2026

1. Introduction

IdeaSwipe ("we", "our", "us") operates the IdeaSwipe Telegram Mini App ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

2. Information We Collect

  • Telegram Account Data: When you launch IdeaSwipe through Telegram, we receive your Telegram user ID, display name, and username via the Telegram WebApp initData mechanism. This data is used solely to identify your account within IdeaSwipe.
  • User-Generated Content: Ideas, sketches, comments, and project data you create within the App. This data is stored to provide the App's core functionality.
  • Pinterest Data: If you connect your Pinterest account, IdeaSwipe accesses your boards and Pins via the Pinterest API on your behalf. We do not store Pinterest data — the API is called in real-time each time you view your content. All Pinterest content is clearly attributed and linked back to its source on Pinterest.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality (idea evaluation, sketching, project management)
  • To authenticate you via Telegram
  • To display your Pinterest boards and Pins within the App (only when you explicitly connect your Pinterest account)
  • To improve the App's user experience

4. How We Do NOT Use Your Information

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • We do not store Pinterest data beyond the current session — we call the Pinterest API each time content is needed
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not require or incentivize users to create or save Pins to any specific website

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Telegram

The App operates within the Telegram platform. Telegram's own privacy policy applies to data processed by Telegram. Learn more at telegram.org/privacy.

6.2 Pinterest

When you connect your Pinterest account, we access your data through the official Pinterest API using OAuth 2.0 authorization. You grant us specific scopes (boards:read, pins:read) and can revoke access at any time through your Pinterest account settings. We comply with the Pinterest Developer Guidelines and API Terms of Service. All Pinterest content displayed in the App links back to its source on Pinterest. Learn more at pinterest.com/privacy.

7. Data Retention

  • User-generated content: Retained until you delete your account or the relevant data
  • Pinterest data: Not stored — retrieved in real-time via API calls
  • Authentication data: Retained while your account is active

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Disconnect your Pinterest account at any time via Pinterest settings
  • Request data portability
  • Object to processing of your data

9. Children's Privacy

IdeaSwipe is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

10. International Users

IdeaSwipe is accessible globally. If you are using the App from outside the jurisdiction where our servers are located, please be aware that your data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in that jurisdiction. By using the App, you consent to such transfer.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

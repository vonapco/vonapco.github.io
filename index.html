How It Works

👆

Swipe to Evaluate

Swipe right to accept ideas, left to reject, up to postpone. A Tinder-style interface for rapid idea assessment.

🎨

Sketch & Visualize

Attach images, links, and visual references to your idea sketches. Build mood boards for your projects.

📋

Organize Projects

Group ideas into projects and folders. Add nested comments and collaborate on your best concepts.

🤖

AI-Powered Scoring

Get real-time AI evaluation of your ideas based on market viability, feasibility, and originality.

📌

Pinterest Integration

Connect your Pinterest account to browse your boards and Pins for visual inspiration within your projects.

💬

Telegram Native

Runs as a Telegram Mini App — no separate download needed. Access your ideas anywhere, anytime.

Pinterest Integration

IdeaSwipe integrates with the official Pinterest API to help users browse their own boards and Pins for visual inspiration. We do not store Pinterest data — the API is called in real-time each time you view your content. All Pinterest content is clearly attributed and links back to its source on Pinterest.

Users can disconnect their Pinterest account at any time through their Pinterest settings.
